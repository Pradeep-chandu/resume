Case Study

Sanar.sa · Maren Solutions · Node.js and TypeScript

Migrating a legacy JavaScript appointment flow to native TypeScript

Described at the level of public behavior. Internal service names, source code, and patient data are omitted.

Problem

A legacy JavaScript appointment flow served web and mobile clients and carried years of business behavior. It needed to move to native TypeScript without changing what clients received.

Verified outcomes

All 125 steps and 19 pipelines reimplemented in native TypeScript, with 66 reusable rules and an automated comparison suite completed before rollout review.

Technical details 6 steps

01Technical challenges

125 ordered processing steps, 19 MongoDB aggregation pipelines with English and Arabic responses, and rules shared across bookings, prescriptions, and documents.

02Architectural approach

Reimplemented the flow step by step in TypeScript and extracted reusable modules: 44 shared business rules and 22 prescription and document rules.

03Implementation strategy

Kept the original step order, request validation, and error handling, so each migrated step could be compared directly with the legacy behavior.

04Database optimization

Migrated and validated all 19 aggregation pipelines, ran independent reads in parallel, used aggregation-backed retrieval, and grouped related bookings to cut redundant work on appointment lists.

05Backward compatibility

Web and mobile response contracts were preserved, so existing clients needed no changes.

06Regression testing

36 HTTP fixtures, 32 production-build pipeline comparisons, and 3,300+ helper and document comparisons against the legacy implementation.
